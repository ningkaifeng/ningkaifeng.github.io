理论简介 / Theory Introduction

治水学（Dynamic Sustenance Theory, DST）立足于黄河文明与大禹治水本土实践，提出存续‑流动统一论，构建“动力‑规则共生”的底层分析框架，识别三浪（技术浪、制度浪、思想浪）与三相（疏浚、方舟、祭祀），由体相诊断逻辑统摄，试图回应新轴心时代下自主知识体系建构的元理论需求。V15.0版本补全组织尺度要素归纳法，建立跨尺度跨学科对接标准，文明尺度升级为四维诊断纲领性框架。

Dynamic Sustenance Theory (DST) is rooted in the Yellow River civilization and the indigenous practice of Yu the Great’s flood‑control. It proposes the Unity of Sustenance and Flow and establishes the foundational framework of "coexistence of dynamics and rules", identifying Three‑Waves (Technological, Institutional, Ideational) and Three‑Phases (Dredging, Arking, Sacrificing), governed by Ti‑Xiang diagnostic logic. Version v15.0 completes the Factor Induction Method for organizational scale, builds cross‑scale & cross‑disciplinary standards, and upgrades civilizational analysis to a four‑dimensional diagnostic schema.

理论核心主张：摒弃自巴门尼德以来静态化的存在哲学传统，将存在理解为动态存续过程；以 Ti‑Xiang Logic（体相逻辑）、Three Waves Mismatch（三波错配）等核心概念，解释复杂系统演化、文明延续、社会秩序与个体生存问题。

Core proposition: Abandon the static ontological tradition since Parmenides, and interpret being as a dynamic process of sustenance. Core concepts including Ti‑Xiang Logic and Three‑Waves Mismatch explain complex‑system evolution, civilizational continuity, social order and individual survival.
