理论定位与核心特征 / Position & Core Features

治水学（Dynamic Sustenance Theory, DST）是一套面向复杂适应系统的通用诊断语法。它回答的问题是：系统如何在永恒的流变中，通过动力与规则的协同，实现自身的存续？

治水学的特点是：
· 起点不可规避（自奠基）——通过践言冲突锚定「存续」为一切实践的终极前提。
· 概念可操作——区分三浪动力（技术、制度、思想）与三元规则（疏浚、方舟、祭祀）；
· 工具可落地——提供从个人七问到组织要素归纳法的完整诊断工具链；
· 尺度可映射——同一套语法在个人、组织、国家、文明四个尺度上均适用。

Dynamic Sustenance Theory (DST) serves as a general diagnostic grammar for complex adaptive systems. It addresses a core question: how can a system achieve Sustenance through the coordination of forces and rules amid perpetual flux?

Core features of DST:
· Falsifiable starting point — anchors Sustenance as an undeniable logical foundation via performative contradiction;
· Operable concepts — distinguishes The Three Waves (Technological, Institutional, Ideational) and The Three Phases: Dredging (Source Transformation), Arking (Boundary Safeguarding), Sacrificing (Consensus Calibration);
· Practical toolkit — provides a complete diagnostic chain ranging from Seven Personal Diagnostic Questions to the organizational Factor Induction Method;
· Scalable mapping — the same grammar applies across four scales: individual, organization, state and civilization.

理论简介 / Theory Introduction

治水学（Dynamic Sustenance Theory, DST）立足于黄河文明与大禹治水本土实践，提出存续‑流动统一论，构建动力‑规则共生的底层分析框架，识别三浪（技术浪、制度浪、思想浪）与三元（疏浚、方舟、祭祀），由体相关系统摄，试图回应新轴心时代下自主知识体系建构的元理论需求。V15.0版本补全组织尺度要素归纳法，建立跨尺度跨学科对接标准，文明尺度升级为四维诊断纲领性框架。

Dynamic Sustenance Theory (DST) is rooted in the Yellow River civilization and the indigenous practice of Yu the Great's flood-control. It proposes the Unity of Sustenance and Flow and establishes the foundational framework of Force-Rule Symbiosis, identifying The Three Waves (Technological Wave, Institutional Wave, Ideational Wave) and The Three Phases: Dredging (Source Transformation), Arking (Boundary Safeguarding), Sacrificing (Consensus Calibration), governed by the ti-xiang relationship. Version v15.0 completes the Factor Induction Method for organizational scale, builds cross-scale & cross-disciplinary standards, and upgrades civilizational analysis to a four-dimensional diagnostic schema.

理论核心主张：摒弃自巴门尼德以来静态化的存在哲学传统，将存在理解为动态存续过程；以体相关系、三浪失配等核心概念，解释复杂系统演化、文明延续、社会秩序与个体生存问题。

Core proposition: Abandon the static ontological tradition since Parmenides, and interpret being as a dynamic process of Sustenance. Core concepts including the ti-xiang relationship and Three-Wave Mismatch explain complex-system evolution, civilizational continuity, social order and individual survival.
